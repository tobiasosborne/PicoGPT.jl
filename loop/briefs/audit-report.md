# Audit of the round 1–2 loop prototypes

- **The positioned prefix floors are correct.** Pass 1 sees two bits. The fixed deep, mixed deep and mixed one-pass floors are respectively `(21/64) ln 2`, `(384975233676617/1653814216454400) ln 2` and `(89539/124740) ln 2`. The training loss gives each length equal weight, then each allowed horizon equal weight within that length. The one-pass population accuracy ceilings are exactly 53.125% and 51.5625% at lengths 32 and 64.
- **The NoPE bounds are correct, and the mixed loss bound can be sharpened.** Repeating the first bit on the left gives full-cone accuracy ceilings of 84.72%, 83.68% and 83.51% at lengths 8, 32 and 64. The reported 0.432345180 nats gives the decoder the word length. Pooling identical padded contexts across lengths, as the shared model must, raises the lower bound to **0.449949952 nats**.
- **All five fitted prefix gain pairs preserve the exact parity sign, fair exterior and a fixed state from pass T onward.** The 16-site Float64 covector probe reproduces its claimed nonzero path. Only its possible support travels one site per reverse pass at arbitrary depth. With the same seed-1 gains and 64 neutral bits, the computed cotangent first becomes identically zero at pass 13 on the way back from pass 64.
- **The Rule 60 targets and reported test matches are correct.** A single impulse and 256 random words establish finite agreement. Separately, every saved two-gain Rule 60 model maps the two sign intervals of magnitude at least 0.99 back into those intervals with the XOR sign. This proves the decoded rule for every binary word and every horizon.
- **The clipped 17-parameter XOR certificates hold for all five seeds on each task.** Their smallest four-pair margins are 1.601379 for prefix parity and 1.591112 for Rule 60. The same certificate covers all five clipped 33-parameter window/deep seeds. The 13-parameter Rule 60 seed 2 misses the four-pair threshold, but a three-state proof also certifies this checkpoint. The stock models' 100% long scores describe their sampled evaluations. Round 1 had one floor hit among 15 width-screen runs. The 270- and 582-parameter normalized round-2 stock recipes each met both criteria in one of five seeds.

The audit used saved parameters, independent context counts, rerun forward and backward passes, and both loop test suites. It did not retrain checkpoints or benchmark their reported timings. [Proofs, qualifications and reproduction commands](../notes/audit.md)
